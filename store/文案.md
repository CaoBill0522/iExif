```
内置 iPhone 4 到最新机型共 96 款设备的镜头预设，选中即自动填好机型、镜头、光圈和焦距。全部在本机完成，不联网。
```

### 描述（4000 字符内）
```
iExif 用来查看和修改照片里保存的拍摄信息，也就是 EXIF 元数据。全部处理都在你的设备上完成，不联网，不上传任何数据。

【设备与镜头预设】
内置 96 款苹果设备的全部镜头，从 iPhone 4 到最新机型，iPad 从第二代到最新款，涵盖超广角、主摄、长焦和前置。选中机型和镜头后，品牌、型号、镜头型号、光圈和焦距会自动填好，不用逐项输入。

预设中的数据取自真机照片的 EXIF，包括镜头型号的完整写法。个别还没有真机样片的镜头会明确标注“估算”，不会让你误以为是实拍数据。

【可以修改的信息】
• 设备：品牌、型号、系统版本
• 镜头：镜头品牌、镜头型号、光圈、焦距、等效焦距
• 拍摄参数：快门速度、ISO、曝光补偿、测光模式、闪光灯、白平衡
• 时间：拍摄时间和时区，精确到秒
• 位置：在地图上点选、搜索地名、使用当前位置，或直接输入经纬度，还可以填海拔和拍摄朝向
• 版权：作者、版权、图片描述

【无损修改】
只改写元数据，不重新压缩图像，画质分毫不变。支持 JPEG、HEIC 和 PNG。

【实况照片】
实况照片保存后仍然是实况照片，视频部分一起保留，视频里的拍摄时间、地点和机型也会同步更新。

【批量修改】
多选照片后统一套用同一组参数。可以整体平移拍摄时间，保留照片之间原有的先后顺序。横向滑过照片即可快速多选，一次最多 100 张。

【地图选点】
在地图上直接选择拍摄地点。中国大陆会自动处理地图坐标与 GPS 坐标的偏差，选中的位置和实际地点一致。

【其他】
• 长按照片放大预览，实况照片会自动播放
• 常用参数可以保存为模板，一键套用
• 可以只修改“照片”App 中显示的时间和位置，不生成新照片
• 保存时可以另存为新照片，或另存后删除原图
• 需要时自动转换为该机型的默认格式，也可以匹配该机型相机的分辨率
• 支持简体中文和英文

【隐私】
不收集任何数据，不联网，没有账号，没有广告，没有内购。照片始终留在你的设备上。
```

### Description
```
iExif lets you view and change the capture information stored inside your photos — the EXIF metadata. Everything happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app never connects to the internet.

DEVICE AND LENS PRESETS
Presets cover 96 Apple devices, from iPhone 4 to the latest models and from iPad 2 to the newest iPads, including every ultra wide, main, telephoto and front camera. Pick a device and a lens, and the make, model, lens model, aperture and focal length are filled in for you.

The preset values come from the EXIF of real photos taken with those devices, including the exact lens model strings. The few lenses with no original sample available yet are clearly marked as estimated.

WHAT YOU CAN CHANGE
• Device: make, model, software version
• Lens: lens make, lens model, aperture, focal length, 35mm equivalent
• Exposure: shutter speed, ISO, exposure compensation, metering mode, flash, white balance
• Date: date taken and time zone, down to the second
• Location: tap the map, search for a place, use your current location, or type coordinates; altitude and direction too
• Rights: artist, copyright, description

LOSSLESS
Only the metadata is rewritten. The image is never re-compressed, so quality stays exactly the same. JPEG, HEIC and PNG are supported.

LIVE PHOTOS
A Live Photo stays a Live Photo. The video is saved alongside it, and its date, location and device info are updated to match.

BATCH EDITING
Apply the same values to many photos at once, or shift all their dates together while keeping their original order. Swipe sideways across photos to select quickly — up to 100 at a time.

MAP PICKER
Choose where a photo was taken right on the map. In mainland China the difference between map coordinates and GPS coordinates is handled automatically, so the pin matches the real place.

MORE
• Touch and hold a photo for a larger preview; Live Photos play
• Save your favorite settings as templates and apply them with one tap
• Change only what the Photos app shows, without creating a new photo
• Save as a new photo, or save and remove the original
• Convert to the device's default format, and match its camera resolution, when you want to
• English and Simplified Chinese

PRIVACY
No data collection, no network access, no account, no ads, no in-app purchases. Your photos never leave your device.
```

## 审核备注（App Review Information → Notes）

```
这是一款完全离线的照片元数据编辑工具，不联网、不收集任何数据、无账号、无内购。

功能说明：
1. 读取所选照片的 EXIF 信息并允许修改，保存时生成一张新照片，原图默认保留。
2. 镜头预设数据来自公开的真机照片 EXIF 统计，用于帮助用户填写字段。
3. 需要照片权限以读取元数据、保存修改后的照片；用户选择“另存并删除原图”时会触发系统删除确认弹窗。
4. 位置权限仅在用户主动点击“当前位置”时使用。

测试方式：打开 App，在图库中选择任意一张照片，即可看到并修改其拍摄信息。无需登录。
```

英文版审核备注：

```
iExif is a fully offline photo metadata editor. It has no network access, collects no data, and has no account or in-app purchases.

How it works:
1. It reads the EXIF of a selected photo and lets the user change it. Saving creates a new photo; the original is kept by default.
2. Lens presets come from published EXIF data of real photos, to help users fill in fields.
3. Photo library access is needed to read metadata and save edited copies. Choosing "Save & Delete Original" triggers the system deletion confirmation.
4. Location access is used only when the user taps "Current Location".

To test: open the app, tap any photo in the library, and edit its capture info. No sign-in required.
```
